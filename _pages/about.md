I'm a PhD student at the University of Washington's Information School, working with Dr. Tanushree Mitra and Dr. Bill Howe.

My research focuses on understanding the epistemic aspects of LLMs, such as how they acquire, represent, and communicate knowledge. I also study how users interact with LLMs and how to design better interfaces for knowledge delivery.

I'm very interested in startups. I currently work as a research engineer at [Gumshoe AI](https://gumshoe.ai), where we build tools to help brands improve their visibility on AI search platforms. I previously co-founded SimLoop AI, an analytics platform that simulates website visits using browser agents. I'm also an EIR at the iStartup Lab at UW, mentoring undergraduate and graduate students on their startup ideas.
